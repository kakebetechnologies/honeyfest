About the Festival

Celebrating Bees, Honey & Our Community

The Gulu Honey Festival is Northern Uganda's most colourful celebration of beekeeping, culture and community. Hosted at Kaunda Ground, the festival brings together farmers, traders, NGOs and nature lovers under one golden sky.

Our purpose is simple — to promote sustainable beekeeping, protect our environment, grow local honey enterprises and connect over 200 beekeepers with buyers, partners and the next generation of agribusiness leaders.

Festival Activities

A Day Full of Sweetness & Culture

From traditional dance to live beekeeping demos — there's something for everyone.

Traditional Dance

Experience the rhythm and energy of authentic Acholi dance performances by local cultural troupes.

Beekeeping Training

Hands-on workshops led by expert apiarists — learn modern hive management and honey harvesting.

Honey Tasting

Sample dozens of honey varieties from across Uganda — discover flavors, aromas and pure quality.

Product Exhibition

Browse and buy raw honey, beeswax candles, propolis, royal jelly and other premium hive products.

Networking

Connect with 200+ beekeepers, NGOs, exporters and partners shaping the future of Ugandan apiculture.

Tree Planting

Join a community tree-planting drive supporting bee forage and a greener Northern Uganda.
